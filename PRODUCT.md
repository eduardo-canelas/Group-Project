# RoutePulse Product Context

## Product Purpose
Delivery operations software for small logistics teams. Dispatchers and fleet managers track loads, manage drivers, and move shipments faster through a real-time web dashboard.

## Register
product

## Users
- Dispatchers: assign and monitor daily loads, watch driver status in real time
- Fleet managers: full oversight of routes, packages, and driver performance
- Drivers: view assigned loads, update package status from the field

## Tone
Operator-grade. Professional, efficient, direct. Tool-first — not consumer-soft. Trust is earned through clarity, not decoration.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS pastel UI (Notion-lite, Linear-pastel)
- Over-animated marketing dashboards
- Heavy glassmorphism as decoration
- Neon-on-black terminal aesthetics

## Brand
RoutePulse — name implies speed and precision tracking. Color accent: teal-to-blue. Logo exists as both a wordmark (routepulse-logo.png) and a mark/icon (routepulse-mark.png).
