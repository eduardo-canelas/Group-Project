# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RoutePulse is used by small-business operators, dispatch admins, and drivers who need clear package accountability during daily delivery work. Admins assign loads, monitor handling events, and resolve exceptions. Drivers need a mobile-first manifest that makes the next package, pickup, drop-off, and status update obvious.

## Product Purpose

RoutePulse tracks packages across drivers, facilities, routes, and handling events. The product exists to reduce lost-package confusion, show who touched what, and give operators a clean command center for package movement.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, exact. The interface should feel like reliable logistics software, not a class-project demo or a decorative startup landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS hero sections, empty glass-card layouts, neon AI gradients, excessive explanation, repeated feature-card grids, and UI that hides operational details behind vague labels. The MP4 auth videos and RoutePulse logo should carry the product story without long marketing copy.

## Design Principles

Show the work moving: package status, route progress, ownership, and recent scans should be visible before any explanation.

Keep actions close to context: admin edits, driver status changes, and auth actions should sit beside the information they affect.

Use confidence through restraint: calm surfaces, precise spacing, direct labels, and a limited accent palette make the system feel trustworthy.

Preserve role clarity: admin and driver screens should feel related, but their priorities must be different.

Design for mobile pressure: drivers may use the app one-handed during a route, so important controls must stay readable and reachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced-motion support, and labels that are explicit enough for screen readers and first-time users.
